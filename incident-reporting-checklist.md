## 4. Actions Taken

-  Reported the incident to IT department.

- Kept all the relevant evidence.

-  Recorded the time of discovery.

- Didn't delete the relevant evidence.

- Followed the organisation's instructions.

-  Recorded any immediate actions taken.

## 5. Evidence

Relevant evidence may include:

- Screenshots.

- Suspicious email messages.

- Error messages.

- System alerts.

- Relevant dates and times.

Do not upload confidential evidence or personal information to a public GitHub repository.

## 6. Reporting

Report the incident to the authorised IT or cybersecurity team using the organisation's official reporting procedure.

## Conclusion

Prompt and accurate incident reporting helps cybersecurity teams investigate threats, reduce damage and prevent similar incidents from occurring again.
